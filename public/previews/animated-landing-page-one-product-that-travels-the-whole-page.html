01 — The stroke

The line is the whole argument.

A gold nib has give. It spreads under pressure and springs back, so a downstroke is heavier than a cross stroke without you doing anything about it. That is the difference between writing and printing, and it is the only reason to own one of these.

03 — Material

Hard rubber, not resin.

Ebonite is rubber vulcanised until it is solid. It is warm to hold, it turns beautifully on a lathe, and it will fade from black to a deep bronze if you leave it in the sun. Most people decide that is the best thing about it.
