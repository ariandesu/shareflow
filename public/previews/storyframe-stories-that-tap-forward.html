The Craft

Details that earn the tap.

Segmented progress

Bars fill in real time and snap to your slide count, so viewers always know how much story is left.

Tap & hold zones

Tap right to advance, left to rewind, press and hold to pause. The gestures everyone already knows.

True full-bleed

Edge-to-edge slides with layered gradients keep captions legible over any photo you throw at it.

Responsive by default

From 390 to 1280 the frame, type, and tap zones rescale cleanly. No awkward in-between states.

Keyboard & a11y

Arrow keys, focus rings, reduced-motion fallbacks, and ARIA roles baked in from the start.

Clean source

One component, zero dependencies beyond your framework. Read it in a minute, own it forever.
