Good to know

The questions that actually decide whether we are the right people for the search.

What if you never find it?

Then we say so and we stop. The fee covers the search, not a guaranteed instrument, and we would rather end a search honestly than place something you will resell in two years.

Do you take anything from the seller?

No, never, and this is the whole reason the firm works the way it does. If we were paid by both sides you could not trust a single thing on this page.

Can you value what I already own?

We can tell you what the papers support and what they do not. A formal valuation for insurance goes to an independent expert, and we will name three.

How long does a search take?

Between four months and two years. The listening takes an afternoon. The waiting for the right instrument to become available is the rest of it.
