Everything you
were going to ask

Will you remove the grain?

Not on a preservation scan, and not on a conservation grade unless the grain is damage rather than stock. Grain is the photograph. Removing it is a decision about the film, so it is yours to make and ours to record.

My reel smells of vinegar.

That is acetate decay and it accelerates. Get it cold and dry today, keep it away from the rest of the collection, and send it to assessment before anything else in the box.

Can you work from a print?

Yes, and often we have to. A surviving print is also the only honest colour reference when the negative has shifted, which is why we ask for one even when we do not scan it.

Who owns the scan?

You do, outright, on delivery. We keep a checksum and the condition report so the work can be audited later, and nothing else.
