04 · Questions

Frequently asked

Is the output actually licensable?

Yes. Nothing is retrieved from a training corpus at generation time, so every stem you export is yours outright, with a signed provenance manifest attached to the session.

Can I bring my own stems in?

Upload up to eight references per session. They condition the take without being reproduced, and the context lock will hold their key and tempo across regenerations.

What happens to an open session offline?

Sessions are local-first. The field, the stem split and the last twelve regenerations stay on your machine, and only the brief is sent when you ask for a new take.

Does it run on a laptop?

The editor does. Generation runs on the cluster, and a session streams its first audio in about 38 ms, so the laptop only ever handles playback and the split.
