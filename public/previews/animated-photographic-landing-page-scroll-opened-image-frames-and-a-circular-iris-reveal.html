Before you book

What if it is cloudy?

Then we do not see anything, and neither do you. Two clouded nights in a row and we refund one of them without being asked. We will not pretend the forecast is better than it is to keep a booking.

Do I need to know anything about astronomy?

No. Someone is on the dome floor every night whose job is to point the thing at whatever you are curious about and answer questions until you run out.

Is there wifi?

In the common room, and deliberately nowhere else. The rooms have no screens because a lit screen ruins your night vision for about twenty minutes and your neighbour's too.

Can I bring my own telescope?

Yes, and there are four concrete piers on the east terrace with power for exactly that. Tell us when you book so one is kept for you.
