Asked at the gate

Can I really save the seed?

Yes, that is the whole point. Everything here is open pollinated, so it breeds true if you keep varieties apart. The catalogue tells you which ones cross and how far apart to grow them.

Why is the germination printed?

Because a count means nothing without it. We test every lot, print the result, and refuse to sell anything under 80 percent rather than quietly pad the packet.

What if the sowing window is wrong for me?

Tell us your postcode at checkout and the window on the packet is set from it. If your ground runs cold, say so and we will move it back a fortnight by hand.

Do you ship abroad?

Within the UK only for now. Seed crosses borders under phytosanitary rules we would rather follow properly than approximate.
