Shader: Halftone

A single ANIMATED WEBGL SHADER: Halftone, dot radius set from the field at each cell centre. The coordinate is floored to a 34-cell lattice, an fbm is sampled once at each CELL CENTRE, and that value sets the dot radius for the whole cell. It is a full-bleed fragment shader with no page around it, about 4KB, no textures, no images and no animation library, and it reacts to the pointer. Drop it behind a hero, a section or a whole page as a living background. Under prefers-reduced-motion it renders one static frame, and it falls back cleanly where WebGL is absent.
