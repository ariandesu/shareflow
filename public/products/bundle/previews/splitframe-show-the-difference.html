How it works

Two images. One handle. Zero fuss.

The component stacks both frames, clips the top one to the handle position, and lets the pointer drive the reveal. It is fully responsive and works with touch, mouse, and keyboard.

01 · Stack

Drop the before and after frames into the same box. Both fill the frame with object-cover.

02 · Clip

The top layer is clipped to a width that tracks the handle. Move it, and the reveal follows.

03 · Drag

Pointer events map x-position to a percentage. Arrow keys nudge it for accessibility.
