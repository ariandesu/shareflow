## Architectural & Aesthetic Breakdown
A standalone timeline COMPONENT for a marketing or product page: A dark ring of nodes that rotates on scroll so the active entry sits at the top. Nodes are placed by rotation and counter-rotated so they never appear tilted. A thin conic arc tracks progress and the hub cross-fades to the active entry. Under reduced motion the ring is removed entirely and a plain vertical list is shown, because a rotating ring with no motion is unusable. Built in the light shadcn register (ground #FAFAFA, cards #FFFFFF, hairline #E4E4E7, one indigo #4F46E5, Inter throughout) so it drops into an existing design system without a restyle. No images, no libraries and no build step; avatars are drawn from initials. About 5KB, responsive to 390px, and it degrades to something genuinely readable under prefers-reduced-motion rather than freezing.
