## Architectural & Aesthetic Breakdown
A single ANIMATED WEBGL SHADER: Warp, the displacement vector drawn as colour. Rather than sampling through the warp, this maps the two components of the displacement vector to two hues and its magnitude to a ridged highlight, so you are looking at the warp itself. It is a full-bleed fragment shader with no page around it, about 4KB, no textures, no images and no animation library, and it reacts to the pointer. Drop it behind a hero, a section or a whole page as a living background. Under prefers-reduced-motion it renders one static frame, and it falls back cleanly where WebGL is absent.
