## Architectural & Aesthetic Breakdown
A single ANIMATED WEBGL SHADER: Metaballs, a summed inverse-square field, thresholded. Five centres on de-synchronised orbits each contribute a small charge over squared distance; the surface is where the sum crosses a threshold, which is why the forms merge and separate like liquid. It is a full-bleed fragment shader with no page around it, about 4KB, no textures, no images and no animation library, and it reacts to the pointer. Drop it behind a hero, a section or a whole page as a living background. Under prefers-reduced-motion it renders one static frame, and it falls back cleanly where WebGL is absent.
