## Architectural & Aesthetic Breakdown
A standalone testimonial COMPONENT for a marketing or product page: Two rows of compact cards scrolling in opposite directions, edge-faded, pausing on hover. Each row duplicates its card set so a translateX of -50% loops seamlessly, with the copy marked aria-hidden. The edge fade is a mask so it works over any background rather than being a gradient overlay matched to one. Built in the light shadcn register (ground #FAFAFA, cards #FFFFFF, hairline #E4E4E7, one indigo #4F46E5, Inter throughout) so it drops into an existing design system without a restyle. No images, no libraries and no build step; avatars are drawn from initials. About 5KB, responsive to 390px, and it degrades to something genuinely readable under prefers-reduced-motion rather than freezing.
