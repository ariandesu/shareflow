## Architectural & Aesthetic Breakdown
A single ANIMATED WEBGL SHADER: Smoke, a two-level domain warp on a hard contrast curve. The same warp as Silk taken through a steep smoothstep and a power curve for near-black troughs against bright crests, plus a filament highlight taken from the warp vector's own magnitude. It is a full-bleed fragment shader with no page around it, about 4KB, no textures, no images and no animation library, and it reacts to the pointer. Drop it behind a hero, a section or a whole page as a living background. Under prefers-reduced-motion it renders one static frame, and it falls back cleanly where WebGL is absent.
