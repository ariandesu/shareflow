Questions

What if it is late?

Then you do not pay for it. Past ninety minutes the run is free, automatically, without you asking or us deciding. It happened eleven times last year out of about nine thousand runs.

What will you not carry?

Anything over twenty kilos, anything living, and anything that will not survive a bike. We will tell you on the phone rather than take the booking and fail it.

Do you subcontract?

No. Twelve riders, all employed, all on the payroll with sick pay. If we cannot cover a run we say no rather than hand it to a gig platform and hope.

Can I track it?

You get a link when the rider collects, and both parties get the photo and the signature the moment it lands. We do not show a live map because it makes people anxious for no benefit.
