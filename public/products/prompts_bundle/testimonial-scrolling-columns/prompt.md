## Architectural Philosophy & Overview
A standalone testimonial COMPONENT for a marketing or product page: Three columns of cards drifting vertically at different speeds, the middle one reversed. Same duplicate-and-translate loop as the marquee but on the vertical axis, with a top and bottom mask. Columns drop out progressively on narrow screens rather than shrinking to unreadable widths. Hover pauses every lane at once. Built in the light shadcn register (ground #FAFAFA, cards #FFFFFF, hairline #E4E4E7, one indigo #4F46E5, Inter throughout) so it drops into an existing design system without a restyle. No images, no libraries and no build step; avatars are drawn from initials. About 5KB, responsive to 390px, and it degrades to something genuinely readable under prefers-reduced-motion rather than freezing.
