Questions worth asking

Why does it not last as long as my usual one?

Because there are no synthetic musks in it. Those are what make a scent sit on skin for twelve hours, and they are also what makes most perfume smell like most other perfume. Ours lasts five to seven hours and then it is gone.

Will it smell the same next year?

Close, but not identical, and we would rather tell you that than pretend. Naturals move with the harvest. The batch number on the base of the bottle tells you which year you have.

Do you test on animals?

No, and we do not sell into any market that would require it. That decision costs us a region and we are not revisiting it.

Can I return a full bottle?

Unopened, within thirty days, yes. Opened, no, which is exactly why we push the sample set first and why it costs less than a third of a bottle.
