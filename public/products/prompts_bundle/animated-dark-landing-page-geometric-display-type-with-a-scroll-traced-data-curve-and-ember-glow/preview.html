Questions from the door

Can I watch without booking?

Yes, Saturdays from ten, standing behind the line. It is loud and it is about forty degrees on the floor in summer, so wear less than you think.

Why does a commission take twelve weeks?

Because the annealer takes fourteen hours per load and it cannot be stacked past a point. Everything else about the process is fast. That oven is the whole schedule.

Will my piece look exactly like the photograph?

No, and anyone telling you otherwise is machine-pressing. Hand-blown work varies by a few millimetres and a shade or two. If you need identical, we will point you to a mould shop.

Do you take beginners?

Shop time is built for them. You will not be handed a pipe and left, and you will not make anything good on the first day, which is the honest part nobody says.
