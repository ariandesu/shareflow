01 — Drift

Every wobble is a note you didn’t play.

Wow and flutter is the deck adding pitch of its own. Belt decks smear it across the whole record. The P-1 measures its own rotation nine hundred times a revolution and corrects inside a single degree of arc.

03 — Arm

Nine inches, two null points.

A pivoted arm can only be tangent to the groove twice. The P-1 arm is cut to Baerwald geometry, so those two points sit where the record is busiest and the error between them never exceeds 1.8 degrees.
