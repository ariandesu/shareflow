01 — Section

The plan is a consequence. The section is the argument.

A plan tells you where the walls are. A section tells you how deep the light gets, how the floor plates step back, and whether the top flat has a terrace or a parapet. We draw the section until it works, then let the plan fall out of it.

03 — Material

Four materials, and none of them are a finish.
